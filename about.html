Our Story

Melghat Honey is sourced from the pristine forests of Melghat, Maharashtra, where indigenous tribal communities have been harvesting honey for generations. Our honey is naturally harvested from wild bee colonies, ensuring the highest quality and purity.

We work directly with tribal communities, providing them with fair compensation and supporting sustainable harvesting practices. Every jar of Melghat Honey represents a commitment to nature, tradition, and community welfare.

  • Wild forest origin · Melghat Tiger Reserve
  • Harvested by indigenous Korku communities
  • Raw, unprocessed & lab-tested purity
Padma Shri Award

India's 4th Highest Civilian Honor

The 1 Rupee Doctors of Melghat

Dr. Ravindra Kolhe & Dr. Smita Kolhe

Honored with the prestigious Padma Shri in 2019 by President Ram Nath Kovind for their extraordinary dedication to tribal healthcare in Melghat, Maharashtra.

Known as the “1 Rupee Doctors,” this remarkable couple has served the tribal communities for over 35 years, charging just one rupee per consultation. Their selfless work has dramatically reduced infant mortality rates and combated severe malnutrition in one of India's most challenging regions.

Their unwavering commitment to healthcare accessibility and community welfare inspired us to support the same communities through ethical honey sourcing — ensuring fair compensation and sustainable livelihoods for tribal families.
