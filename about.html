Our Story

From Forest to Your Home

Pure wild forest honey · Harvested by tribal communities of Melghat · Empowering livelihoods since 1999

Pragati Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha

Rooted in Melghat Forests

Melghat Honey is the proud brand of Pragati Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha (PBS), a registered public charitable trust and society working since 1999 in the tribal heartland of Maharashtra. We work directly with indigenous communities — primarily Korku and other tribes of the Melghat region — who have harvested wild rockbee honey for generations.

Under the Government of India’s SFURTI (Scheme of Fund for Regeneration of Traditional Industries) programme of the Khadi & Village Industries Commission (KVIC), we established a dedicated beekeeping cluster. Our focus has always been on non-destructive, scientific and sustainable harvesting that protects rockbee colonies while delivering pure, high-quality honey.

  • Wild forest origin · Melghat Tiger Reserve region
  • Harvested by trained tribal honey hunters
  • Raw · Unprocessed · Lab-tested purity
  • Exported to Dubai & Japan for years
Tribal Empowerment

Over 800 tribals trained in scientific honey collection. 2000+ artisans trained across 11 districts of Vidarbha with support from TRIFED & TDC. Hundreds of Self-Help Groups formed.

Sustainable Harvesting

Traditional crude methods destroyed colonies. We introduced nature-friendly techniques so that rockbee colonies thrive while communities earn a dignified livelihood from honey & beeswax.

From Forest to World

Melghat Honey is sold across major Indian cities and has been regularly exported to Dubai and Japan — a testament to its unmatched purity and quality.

National Excellence Award

Honoured by the President of India

National Award for Excellence in Khadi & Village Industries · 2005

Dhanraj Koche

Pragati Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha, Wardha (Maharashtra)

Conferred the prestigious National Excellence Award 2005 by Hon’ble President of India Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam at the National Convention of Small Scale & Rural Industries, New Delhi (28 October 2005).

The award was given for outstanding excellence in the field of Khadi and Village Industries — recognising our pioneering work in scientific honey collection, processing and marketing with tribal communities of Melghat.

This remains one of the highest national recognitions received by any organisation working in the traditional forest-honey sector.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To promote natural living by providing pure, unadulterated forest honey while empowering tribal communities through fair trade, skill training and sustainable harvesting practices.

Our Vision

To create lasting avenues for sustainable livelihoods for rural and tribal populations through collection of honey, beeswax and other bee products, backed by strong branding and ethical marketing.

Our Values

Authenticity, sustainability, community empowerment and complete transparency. We believe every jar should honour the forest, the bees and the hands that harvest it.
